## The kinds

A reverie is one of: **SLEEP** (today's sleep sounds), **MEDITATION**, **NATURE** (immersion: rain, wind, surf, a
forest -- PulseEntrain's procedural textures and made pieces), **TINCTURES** (the simulations). More may come; a kind is
a label, a default drift and a default way of making its music, nothing more.

## The drift is a file now

DRIFT DOWN today is one fixed ramp -- 10 Hz, then 6, then 2 -- which is why it always feels the same. A drift becomes
a **PulseEntrain session (`.imed` v2, PulseEntrain `docs/SESSION_FORMAT.md`)**: a timeline of scenes, each with its
beat and carrier, ramped between, with noise beds and textures held forward; its light and stimulation axes are kept
and ignored here. The player picks a premade drift or one made on the site; the phone plays it under the music
(headphones), as DRIFT DOWN does now. The old ramp becomes one drift among many ("THE LONG FALL", DRAFT).

**The starter library** is PulseEntrain's 93 programs (2.3 MB): Pure, Dream Chill, Focus and Energy, Emotions,
Spirituality, Sex and Intimacy and Experimental as they are, and the 36 of Pharmacy renamed as TINCTURES by how they
feel. PulseEntrain is the user's own; it is carried over, not re-derived.

## Making one, in the app

REVERIE (where SLEEP SOUNDS is now): choose a kind, then a drift (premade, the site's, or the kind's default), then the
music -- a piece already on the hub, one made now from the daemon's context (sleep sounds' recipe, widened per kind), or
none. Play it; keep it. The watch plays a reverie's render from its card, as it plays sleep sounds now.

## The site: downdrift.daemons.codemusic.ca

PulseEntrain's Studio, carried over: the scene timeline, each scene's beat and carrier, the beds. A music track is
chosen from the hub's library or made there with the same code the app asks. Saved to the hub as a drift, and -- if
asked -- as a finished render.

## Space

The hub keeps everything (28 GB, 24 free on 2026-11-24). roverbyteseer runs the site and the music maker but **keeps
nothing** once the hub has fetched a piece, as sleep sounds already does (27 GB free there; the user does not want it
filling). Drifts are kilobytes; music and renders are the cost: a render is made on asking, not by default; the hub
counts REVERIE's bytes in THE ENGINE ROOM; past a limit the oldest unplayed renders go first (their drift and music are
kept, so a render can always be made again). If space becomes a problem, the hub's part is reviewed with the user.
