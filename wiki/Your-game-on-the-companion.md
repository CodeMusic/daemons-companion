# Your game on the companion

The companion reads your save copy, so it can show you what you have done in the game -- on the app, the site and the
Tab5 alike. **It never spells out what your save has not reached**: a MARK not yet earned, a grove not yet found, a
story event still to come or a book not yet in your hands shows as not yet, and nothing more.

How the game itself is played -- the world, daemons, battles and the types, MARKS and ROOTS, the INDEX, the NOTEBOOK,
the books -- is in
[the DAEMONS player's manual](https://github.com/CodeMusic/DAEMONS/blob/main/docs/DAEMONS-manual.pdf).

A phone hosting on its own reads its own copy the same way
([Host on your phone](Host-on-your-phone#or-the-phone-on-its-own-c-111)), and a paired phone keeps all of it, so YOU
and the LIBRARY open away from home too ([Away from home](Away-from-home)). Every word on these screens is a draft, as
the game's are.

| tab | what of your game it holds |
|---|---|
| **TODAY** | **YOUR GAME** -- where you last saved, how many MARKS you hold, your MONEY and what the CASINO has waiting for the next SYNC -- and **IN THE LIBRARY**, what waits there; each opens its place |
| **DAEMON** | the party as the game draws it, the **INDEX**, **SOUNDINGS**, **DREAMS** and, once the book is held, **READS** |
| **LIBRARY** | THE PLAYER'S MANUAL, CALLOW SCHOOL, THE GUIDE, THE SUBTLE LAYER, the NOTEBOOK, the SONGBOOK and STREAM |
| **YOU** | you as the game draws you, the save's facts, the MARKS and ROOTS, the region maps, GROVES, GAME EVENTS, **THE ROAD AHEAD** and **TRADE** ([Trading](Trading)) |

## YOU

At the top, **you as the game draws you** (C-125): the trainer card's picture and your walking frame, REASON or
INSTINCT as you chose, your name, edition and ID No., your rival, where you last saved, and whether this is **your
companion's game** (the first save you SYNC becomes it). Beside them, **the two region maps** (C-127): the mainland and
the islands, each place lit once your save has visited it, a route lit when it touches a lit place. The islands' name
reads ??? and their map stays dark until the story is told to the end.

Then the facts: PLAY TIME, **MONEY** (and who is at the CASINO now), SHOES, the INDEX, the **MARKS**, the DIPLOMA,
OPUS, THE STORY once it is told to the end, and the **ROOTS**.

### MARKS

The eight MARKS are the trainer card's own badges, faint until earned, with a count of how many you hold (C-126). Tap
one you have earned and it opens as a small page (C-140):

- its badge and its name, the first line of its lesson leading and the rest as points, and the town whose BENCHMARK
  gave it;
- **LEADER, AFTERWARDS** -- what its leader says once it is earned: where to come back -- and then, once its
  understanding is held, **LEADER, WITH** that understanding instead (C-133);
- **UNION PLUGIN** -- its name, its routine and what it does, once it has been given;
- **INSIGHT** with its virtue, as a quote, once the insight has arrived.

A MARK not yet earned says only that.

### ROOTS

Each understanding is a ROOT in its own colour, filled once it is held. Tap one and it opens: its name in its colour,
its lesson (the STREAM lecture the game gives it), where it was arrived at, **PAIRED WITH** its MARK once that is
earned, and the insight once it has arrived. A ROOT not yet held says *Not yet.*

### GROVES

There is no GROVES card until your save has found a grove (C-132). Then each grove found is listed as the universe it
is; tap one for its sign and who lives there. Nothing is counted or listed ahead.

### GAME EVENTS

The story so far, in finer detail than the facts (C-128), by section, in story order -- the same rows the game's
testing build keeps. A row your save holds is marked **+** and named; one still ahead is **·** *Still to come.*, and
not named; **?** means the save cannot say.

## The LIBRARY

A shelf of what can be read (C-145, C-156), each book opening as a page of its own.

- **THE PLAYER'S MANUAL** (C-233) -- first on the shelf, for everyone: DAEMONS' own manual (the PDF in DAEMONS' `docs/`,
  rebuilt at every release), every way to play and how it all works, telling how and never where. On the iPhone and
  iPad it is fetched from DAEMONS on GitHub as published, kept on the device (so it opens away from home and offline),
  fetched again when a newer one is out, and read in the app's own page, scrolled and pinched as any PDF; **Share**
  keeps it in Books or Files. On the site it opens in the page, or in a tab of its own. None of it is in this
  repository.
- **CALLOW SCHOOL** (C-129) -- your **DIPLOMA** in the game's certificate words, with your best mark and its letter;
  without one, how many times you have sat the paper and your best. The seven floors, each opening its syllabus and its
  board once you have stood on it (greyed and closed until then). And **THE PAPER, TO SIT AGAIN**: the whole paper, a
  question at a time, marked here with the letter and every miss shown with its answer. **It is practice: your save is
  never written.**
- **THE GUIDE** -- only once your save has found it. Its 8-bit cover (its title and author set in the app's own type --
  the art carries none of the game's lettering, which is Nintendo's), CONTENTS by part, and each chapter on the book's
  own dark page, with BACK, CONTENTS and NEXT; its last chapter ends on READ, the link its QR code opens. **ASK THE
  GUIDE** (C-209): type a question, or hold and speak it, and the book answers from its own pages
  ([The hub and its AI](The-hub-and-its-AI)). Nothing of it shows before it is found.
- **THE SUBTLE LAYER** (DAEMONS T-423) -- a trainer's handbook, only once your save has found it. An open book in its
  own purples: its cover (its title set in the app's own type), the contents, then each page with its picture on the
  left and its words on the right (one above the other on a phone); a few pictures turn, and in TWO VOICES the second,
  asking voice is printed faint. What the two books are: the DAEMONS wiki's
  [Books](https://github.com/CodeMusic/DAEMONS/wiki/Books).
- **Reads and leans** (C-203, the game's T-459) -- once your save holds THE SUBTLE LAYER, the watches start to notice.
  Now and then the daemon on the face stops, turns, and a purple ripple rises from it: something is there (a stranger's
  daemon heard far off, rain on the way). Now and then a purple **"A read:"** comes with a soft tap -- someone close by,
  rain before long, a long still night, something to the north on a walk -- and at the CASINO's reels, sometimes, **"A
  lean:"** while they turn. Some reads are about nothing, worded the same. The companion judges each by what happens
  after and keeps the count on the DAEMON page's **READS**; it never says a read was right before it lands. The NOTEBOOK's
  **READS -> THE LOG** is the game's own, written here from your save as the game writes it. Every word DRAFT.
- **ASK THE SUBTLE LAYER** (C-229, C-230) -- the GUIDE answers as REASON would; THE SUBTLE LAYER as INSTINCT would. In
  the book (the LIBRARY) and on the Ultra's BOOKS: **ASK** -- a line from its nearest page, then a question back, faint, as
  the book prints its asking voice; **TWO VOICES** -- say a thing, and it only asks what it means; **TODAY'S QUESTION** --
  one a day, set in the day's centre, word and note, and what you answer is kept in its journal with the day and the
  centre. Typed, or held and spoken; read aloud where the voice is. On a phone alone the line is the book's own sentence;
  with the hub, its module may say it in its own words. The GUIDE, for its part, ends some answers on the day's virtue
  over its shadow. Every word DRAFT.
- **THE SUBTLE LAYER's cards** (C-232) -- twenty-two, our own arcana, each with two faces on the book's purple: **REASON**,
  a circuit board under green with a daemon of the mainland (where the game teaches computer science and machine
  learning), and **INSTINCT**, a living net under gold with a daemon of the islands (where it teaches the mind) -- a
  keyword at the top, a phrase under the daemon; now and then a **shiny** one, its colours inverted, a gold foil edge.
  **THE DAY'S CARD** (the same all day, kept beside the day's answer in the journal), **DRAW A CARD** whenever you want
  one -- one each, with a friend -- and **PAST · PRESENT · FUTURE**. In the book and on the watches' BOOKS. A card points
  you toward something to notice; it never says what will happen. Every word DRAFT.
- **Late at night** -- some say their daemon is not quite itself in the small hours: a shimmer that was not there, a
  shadow that is not there, a routine that is in no list. The companion does not explain it, and neither does this page.
- **A third book?** -- there is talk of one among the LIBRARY's books, a grimoire with cards of its own, said to bend
  the rules the other two keep, and of a day in October that is stranger with it than without. What is said of it, and
  the real history behind it: [THE HERMETIC LAYER](https://github.com/CodeMusic/DAEMONS/wiki/The-Hermetic-Layer), on
  the DAEMONS wiki.
- **Words under words** (C-204) -- with THE SUBTLE LAYER held, four of the daemon's own lines carry a second, in purple, after
  it: what it means beneath what it says. You chose them: under "Hi! I missed you!", *I counted.*; under "Goodnight.
  Stay near?", *Don't go far.*; under "Again! Again!", *Watch me.*; under "Five more minutes?", *Stay with me.* On the
  boards it comes where the line was, as it goes; in the app, under it. Every word DRAFT.
- **The NOTEBOOK** -- every page your save holds (C-125), in the game's own sections, each tapped open and laid out as
  the game's NOTEBOOK lays it. A dot marks a page you have not yet read in the game; an open page says where it was
  found. A page the game writes from your save each time you open it there says so, rather than guessing. **A page that
  ends on a QR code in the game** has a button instead (C-146): **Listen** for a song, **Read** for anything else, and
  it opens the link. On the Tab5, which has no browser, the code itself is drawn under the page, to scan with a phone.
- **The SONGBOOK** -- every song your save has learned (C-152): its key, a dot in the key's colour, and its motif on a
  treble staff, each note's head in the colour of its pitch, as the game draws it -- the twelve key colours and the
  outline exported from the game -- with a sharp or a flat beside it and ledger lines where needed. **LISTEN** plays it,
  each note held as long as the game holds it, on your game's own instrument: the HARP for a CONTENT save, the FLUTE for
  CONTEXT ([Settings](Settings#sound)).

  **Your instrument, to play** (DAEMONS T-426) -- on the phone and the site, the SONGBOOK has the instrument itself,
  **LOCKED** until the game has given it to you. PLAY FREELY, or choose a song (one learned, or one only heard, to
  practise), then a key from the wheel. The HARP is eight strings to pluck or sweep a finger across; the FLUTE eight
  holes held while you breathe into the microphone (a finger alone where there is none); an octave either way. Each
  note drifts up in its key's colour as the game draws it, and the companion listens as the game does: a learned song
  in its key joins on the staff and echoes, and the right notes in another key are ALMOST. A song only heard is to be
  played back in the game. On the phone the harp takes every finger, for a chord or a strum. SETTINGS' **PREVIEW**
  shows it (and THE SUBTLE LAYER) as if the game had given it, to try before then -- your save is never touched.
- **STREAM** -- every lesson a MARK or a ROOT has opened, read as their pages read them: its first line leading and the
  rest as points, and where it was opened.

**READ ALOUD** (C-208) is on every chapter of THE SUBTLE LAYER and THE GUIDE and every NOTEBOOK page with words: a
paragraph at a time, in the INDEX voice, the next asked for while one plays. The voice needs the companion; a phone
alone has none. A watch reads the same books from its BOOKS ([The T-Watch Ultra](The-T-Watch-Ultra)).

On the Tab5 the LIBRARY holds the same books; THE GUIDE and THE SUBTLE LAYER open full screen, the SONGBOOK's LISTEN
plays on its speaker, and **PLAY THE HARP** (or FLUTE) is there by touch -- the strings touched or drawn across, the holes
held (the finger alone: its microphones are TALK's), each note on a speaker channel of its own so plucks ring together.

## The INDEX

In DAEMON, **INDEX**: every entry your save has seen, in your edition's voice, with **LISTEN** to hear it read aloud
([Talk to your daemon](Talk-to-your-daemon)), and OPUS's margin beside it where your save holds OPUS. Each entry also
says what its daemon is **NATIVE** to, in the game's own words and order (C-143): a region or a grove's universe,
CONTENT ONLY or CONTEXT ONLY, its hours, its seasons, NOT AT or NOT IN, or NOT IN THE WILD.

**The cards in their colours** (C-186): in the app, an INDEX card is the whole card -- number, types and their
colours, height and weight, both editions' entries, NATIVE, OPUS's margins -- and it takes its colour as your save comes
to understand that kind of daemon. Until then it stays grey.

**On the T-Watch Ultra**, THE INDEX is first in BOOKS (C-199): every daemon your save has met, its picture, kind and
entry, and LISTEN -- kept on the watch's card, so it opens with no network ([The T-Watch Ultra](The-T-Watch-Ultra)).

## SOUNDINGS

In DAEMON, **SOUNDINGS** (C-195; the game's T-457 and T-458): one quiet card for each daemon away, with when it was last
heard and how it was doing then -- *No signal yet*, *Quiet today*, *Doing fine*, *Doing well* (DRAFT). Each SYNC writes
it into your save, in the spare bits of that daemon's own record, for the game to read. Nothing to fix, no score and no
streak: only what was heard.

## DREAMS

In DAEMON, **DREAMS**: the journal of every night a watch told the companion, and the dream your daemon had. At a SYNC
the last one goes into your save for the game. All of it: [Nights and dreams](Nights-and-dreams).

## THE ROAD AHEAD

In YOU, **THE ROAD AHEAD** (C-191): from where your save is, the next step and where; the fights and trainers not yet
met, by place; the levels so far; the places open to you, and each one's wild daemons in your edition. **Nothing past
the next step** -- it counts the story's steps your save has done and shows only what is at or under that count, and
holds no secret and no hidden book. Its words are DRAFT.

## The CASINO's coins

**THE CASINO plays with your game's money** -- on the boards, the phone, the site and the Tab5 alike (C-149, C-155,
[The CASINO](The-CASINO)). Until the next SYNC, YOU says beside MONEY what has been won or lost, over how many spins,
and what your MONEY will be after, and TODAY says it under YOUR GAME. SYNC writes it into the save after the backup
every SYNC makes -- never below nothing, never past 999999 -- and says what it brought: *The CASINO won ... over ...
spins: MONEY ... to ...* The Tab5 shows the same beside its MONEY.

Its reels show daemons your save has SEEN in the INDEX, so as you play the game, new ones come to the reels.

## Light, dark or auto

Every screen comes in LIGHT and DARK, and AUTO is the default (C-144): on the site and the phone it follows what the
computer or the phone is set to; on the Tab5, which has no such setting, AUTO is dark from 8pm to 6am. In the dark the
MARK badges sit on a chip of paper and a ROOT's colour is lifted until it reads. The choice is on SETTINGS
([Settings](Settings#in-the-app)).
